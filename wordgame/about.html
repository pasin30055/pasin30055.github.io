This game, invented by Prof. Dana Moshkovitz and implemented by Pasin Manurangsi, is inspired by research in hardness of approximation. In short, the game is a formulation of the projection games problem (also known as Label Cover), a problem of great significance in hardness of approximation.


To learn about projection games, please check out Prof. Moshkovitz's presentation on the topic.
