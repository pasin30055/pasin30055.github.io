This game, invented by Prof. Dana Moshkovitz and implemented by Pasin Manurangsi, is inspired by research in hardness of approximation. In short, the game is a formulation of the projection games problem (also known as Label Cover), a problem of great significance in hardness of approximation. The initial implementation was done in 2013, with some small updates in 2026.


To learn about projection games, please check out Prof. Moshkovitz's presentation on the topic.
